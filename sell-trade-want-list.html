Collector Requests

Sell, trade, or tell DJ what you are hunting.

Use this page when you have a collection to sell, a trade idea, or a want list for cards, comics, autographs, memorabilia, and other collectibles.

Send Your DetailsMessage on Facebook

Sell cards, comics, or collectibles

Send the item type, player or title, year, brand or publisher, quantity, condition notes, and whether the collection is raw, slabbed, signed, sealed, or mixed.

Trade with DJ

Share what you have, what you are looking for, and whether you prefer a straight trade, partial cash deal, or a conversation about value first.

Send a want list

Tell DJ the players, teams, comics, eras, grades, price ranges, or specific cards you are chasing. Listing examples help narrow the search quickly.

Photos that help

Front and back photos are best for cards. For comics, include the front cover, back cover, spine, staples, and any major flaws. For memorabilia, include signatures, certificates, tags, and closeups.

Condition notes

Call out creases, surface wear, rounded corners, writing, stains, trimming concerns, loose staples, cracked slabs, or anything else you would want to know as a buyer.

What happens next

DJ reviews the details, asks follow-up questions if needed, and usually replies within 24 hours. No deal is assumed until both sides are comfortable.

Ready to send it?

Start with the basics and DJ can take it from there.

Include your name, best contact method, photos if you have them, and whether you want to sell, trade, consign, make an offer, build a bundle, or ask DJ to find something. If you are responding to a site listing, include the Listing ID or product link.
