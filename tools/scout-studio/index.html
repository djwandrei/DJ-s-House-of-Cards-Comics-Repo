Know what the evidence means.

Observed

Structured event statistics and reconstructed shared-floor possessions. Always check attempts, minutes and coverage.

Not measured here

Gravity, decision speed, screen navigation and defender assignments cannot be read from steals, blocks or shot percentages.

Not a forecast

These views do not predict wins, health, a player's career or how an untested lineup would perform. Adjusted model coefficients stay private.
