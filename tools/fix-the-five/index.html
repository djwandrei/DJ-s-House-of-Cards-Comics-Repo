Rules & board guide
How your board result works

Make the move. Read the tradeoff.

  1. Choose a legal swapPick one replacement that fits the disclosed role rules for this daily board.
  2. Lock the moveYour result stays hidden until you commit, so each choice has a real reveal.
  3. Read the board resultSee a 0–100 board score and rank against the legal replacements on this one board.

The validated Scout model determines the board-only comparison. Public historical stats identify the player and source; private values, coefficients, and archive details are never sent to the browser.

Board terms and scoring boundary
Source-labeled board
A fixed player pool from one disclosed source window. It is not a current depth chart.
Legal swap
A replacement that meets the round’s stated role and board rules.
Sealed board rank
Your place among only the legal choices on this board after you lock a move—not a career rating or forecast.
Local best
Your strongest completed run saved only in this browser. It never changes your account or the shop.

Today’s rotation can use a single team-season, a franchise across validated seasons, or a randomized multi-season player pool. If a validated source is unavailable, the game does not substitute an old board or a non-Scout score.
