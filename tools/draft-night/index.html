How the board works

Draft, lock, then reveal.

  1. Understand the boardThe board fixes the available player pool, role requirements, and comparison rules.
  2. Draft a legal fiveChoose one player from each displayed source-listed role group.
  3. Lock your picksYour exact five stays unrevealed until all five roles are filled.
  4. Read the board resultSee a 0–100 result and rank among legal combinations on this board only.

The validated Scout model supplies the board-only comparison. Public historical stats identify the player and source; private values and coefficients never reach the browser.

Board terms and scoring boundary
Source-labeled board
A fixed player pool, role set, and comparison rule from one disclosed source window.
Legal path
One five-player combination satisfying every role rule on this board.
Board rank
Your place among the legal paths after all five picks, not a team rating or forecast.
