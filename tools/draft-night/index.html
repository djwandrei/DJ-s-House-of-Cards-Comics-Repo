Rules & board guide
How your board result works

Draft, lock, then reveal.

  1. Draft a legal fiveChoose one player from each disclosed source-listed role group.
  2. Lock the boardYour exact five stays unrevealed until all five picks are in.
  3. Read the board resultSee a 0–100 board score and rank across legal combinations on this board only.

The validated Scout model determines the board-only comparison. Public historical stats identify the player and source; private values, coefficients, and archive details are never sent to the browser.

Board terms and scoring boundary
Source-labeled board
A fixed player pool from one disclosed source window. It is not a current depth chart.
Legal path
One five-player combination that satisfies every role rule on this board.
Sealed board rank
Your place among the legal paths after you lock all five picks—not a team rating, player rating, or forecast.
Local best
Your strongest completed draft saved only in this browser. It never changes your account or the shop.

Today’s board can use a single team-season, a franchise across validated seasons, or a randomized multi-season player pool. If a validated source is unavailable, the game does not substitute an old board or a non-Scout score.
