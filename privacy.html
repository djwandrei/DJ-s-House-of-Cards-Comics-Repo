Privacy Policy

Effective June 13, 2026

Information used by the store

Customer accounts use Supabase authentication. Secure checkout is handled by Stripe. Those services process the information needed to authenticate customers, accept payment, prevent fraud, and connect orders to the correct buyer.

Local browser information

Your shopping cart and wishlist may be stored in your browser. Signed-in wishlist and buyer-profile information may also synchronize to the customer account. Clearing browser storage can remove locally stored information.

Contact messages

Information submitted through contact or inquiry tools is used to respond to questions, offers, sales, trades, and order concerns.

Sharing and retention

Information is shared only with service providers needed to operate the store, process checkout, and communicate with customers. Payment-card details are handled by Stripe and are not stored in this website's browser code.

Your choices

Contact djscardscomics13@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests concerning your customer information.
