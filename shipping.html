Store Policies

Shipping Policy

Effective June 18, 2026

DJ's House of Cards & Comics ships cards, comics, and collectibles with packaging chosen for the item type, value, and condition. Checkout shows the available shipping charge before payment, and shipping options are organized around the four weight tiers below.

Tier 13 oz or under

Best for many single raw-card orders and other very light, protected shipments.

Tier 28 oz

Used for heavier card shipments, many graded-card orders, and compact protected bundles.

Tier 312 oz

Used when the item or bundle needs more packing material but still fits below the 1 lb tier.

Tier 41 lb

Used for comics, larger bundles, memorabilia, and heavier collectibles that fit the configured store tier.

Shipping destinations and charges

Secure checkout currently collects United States shipping addresses. The checkout page controls the final shipping charge, available service, taxes, and order total before payment is submitted.

Combined orders

When multiple items are purchased together, the shipment may move into a higher weight tier so the order can be packed safely. If a bundle needs manual review, contact DJ before checkout.

Packaging

Raw cards, graded slabs, comics, autographs, and memorabilia are packed differently. DJ uses sleeves, top loaders, team bags, boards, rigid protection, padding, or boxes when appropriate for the item.

Processing and tracking

Orders are prepared after payment confirmation. Tracking information is provided when the selected shipping service supports it, and carrier scans may take time to appear after a label is created.

Address accuracy and delivery issues

Review the shipping address before completing checkout. Contact DJ promptly if a package is delayed, arrives damaged, or appears delivered incorrectly so the available carrier and order records can be reviewed.
