Condition & Authenticity

Know what you are looking at before you buy.

Cards, comics, autographs, memorabilia, and raw collectibles all deserve clear condition notes before money changes hands. If a listing leaves you wondering, ask before checkout and DJ will help verify the details.

Ask a Condition QuestionShipping Notes

Raw cards and comics

Raw items are described with visible condition details when known, including wear, corners, edges, centering, surface, creases, writing, staples, covers, and other collector-relevant notes. Photos are part of the condition description.

Graded and slabbed items

When an item is slabbed, the listing should show the grading company and grade exactly as presented. PSA, BGS, SGC, CGC, and similar labels are referenced only when they apply to that specific item.

Autographs and memorabilia

Signatures, relics, and memorabilia are represented according to the listing details available. DJ will not overpromise authentication beyond the item data, slab, certificate, manufacturer note, or visible evidence tied to the piece.

Final-sale condition review

All sales are final, so condition-sensitive buyers should ask for more photos or clarification before buying. Raw items are not guaranteed to receive any future grade, even when they look strong in photos.

Careful packaging

Cards, comics, and collectibles are packed with care for the item type. Slabs, raw cards, comics, and memorabilia may need different protection, and packing choices follow the needs of the item.

Order concerns

If the wrong item arrives or a package is damaged in transit, contact DJ within 3 calendar days with photos of the item, packaging, and label so the issue can be reviewed directly.
